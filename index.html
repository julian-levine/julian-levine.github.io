About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Teaching, Learning, and Educational Improvement (TLEI) cluster at the UCI School of Education. As a member of the Digital Learning Lab, I conduct research on the Converse to Learn project, exploring language and literacy development through technology.

I hold a B.A. in economics from UC San Diego and an M.A. in education from UC Berkeley, specializing in social research methodologies.

In collaboration with my brother, I create animated music videos for children learning English.
